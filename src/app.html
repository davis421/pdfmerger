Tenant PDF Merger

Combine ID photos, application reports, screening reports and bank statements into one PDF. Everything happens on this computer, and nothing is uploaded.

  1. Add the files. It groups them by tenant and puts them in order automatically.
  2. Check the groups. Choose a tenant for each ID photo. Drag the ⋮⋮ handle to move a file, even into another tenant, or to move a whole tenant.
  3. Preview the pages. See every page in its final order, rearrange, rotate or leave out pages, then download.
Drop files here, or click to choose PDFs and photos (JPG, PNG). You can add more at any time.
How it sorts and what it handles

Tenants come from the applicant name on each application or screening report. Bank statements and other PDFs go to the tenant whose name appears on them. Photos can't be read reliably, so you choose their tenant, unless there's only one tenant.

Within each tenant the order is: ID photo, application report, screening report, bank statement, pay stubs, other. Drag or use the arrows to change it.

Photos are turned upright automatically. Use Rotate if a photo still looks sideways. Statements locked against editing are unlocked so they can merge. A file that needs a password to open can't be merged.
